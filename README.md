# Feed Insights

I want to build a prototype that probably should look like a very simple Instagram feed of, uh, yeah, uh, somebody's Instagram feed. What I want to explore is, like, I want to put a brand new "Why am I seeing this?" button, like, that looks kind of cool and present, and when you click on it, I think it should just show you all of the decisions algorithm made in a very clear, really a human user-friendly way. It should show you all of the decisions algorithm made, all of the data algorithm used to show you what it has decided to show you the, these posts. And I think the posts don't have to be advertising only, which is also important. But I think it should also show you, like, why are you seeing this photo from your very old friend? Why are you seeing this photo from a brand? Why are you seeing this photo from an influencer? Why are you seeing this, uh, advertising? So I think I need to have, like, a multiple examples, and I want this place, like, you know, it shows, why am I seeing this? And you click on it, it kind of expands and basically shows you all of the decisions it has made, and maybe it even gives you a little, uh, quick access to a different place where you can adjust your algorithm somehow. I think adjusting your algorithm part can be a second different prototype, so we don't need to design this right now. Uh, but let's just make this feed and see how we like that.
